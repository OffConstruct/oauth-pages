Privacy policy

niche-lead-engine is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, and it runs on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads messages in the owner's Gmail mailboxes, sends one summary email from the owner's main mailbox to that same mailbox, and creates drafts in the owner's outreach mailbox. It never sends a draft by itself.

Where data goes

Data read through Google APIs stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and it is not used for advertising. Text may be passed to an AI model under the owner's own account only to write notes and drafts for the owner. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local data folder.
